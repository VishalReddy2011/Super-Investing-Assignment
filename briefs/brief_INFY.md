# Infosys Limited (NSE: INFY) - Equity Research Brief
*As of 23 September 2026*

## 1. Snapshot
Infosys Limited is a global leader in next-generation digital services, consulting, and outsourcing. The company provides a comprehensive suite of services including AI-led digital transformation (Infosys Topaz), cloud computing (Infosys Cobalt), engineering services, and enterprise platforms like Finacle. As of September 2026, the company maintains a robust market capitalization of approximately ₹4.41 lakh crore, with a P/E ratio of 14.82 and healthy operating margins of 21.17%.

## 2. Bull Case
*   **AI Leadership:** Through "Infosys Topaz," the company is effectively capturing enterprise demand for Generative AI, positioning itself as a primary partner for large-scale digital transformation projects.
*   **Resilient Business Model:** Despite recent anxieties regarding US immigration policies (PERM suspension), the company has officially stated that it does not expect a material impact, signaling a strong, localized global workforce strategy.
*   **Diverse Revenue Base:** Infosys services a vast array of industries—ranging from financial services and retail to life sciences and manufacturing—which provides a significant buffer against cyclical downturns in any single sector.
*   **Strong Platform Ecosystem:** The company’s focus on proprietary platforms like Finacle (banking) and EdgeVerve creates sticky client relationships and recurring revenue streams beyond traditional time-and-material contracts.

## 3. Bear Case
*   **Geopolitical/Regulatory Risk:** Increased scrutiny on US immigration and visa policies, while currently manageable, poses a perpetual risk to the company’s traditional offshore-to-onshore service delivery model.
*   **Client Spending Caution:** Macroeconomic uncertainty in Western markets (particularly the US and Europe) may lead clients to pause or delay large-scale discretionary IT spending, impacting short-term growth.
*   **Margin Compression:** Constant pressure to invest in high-cost AI infrastructure and top-tier talent, combined with wage inflation, could pressure operating margins if the company is unable to pass costs on to clients.
*   **Intense Competition:** The IT services sector is highly fragmented and competitive, with aggressive pricing from mid-tier players and global consulting giants forcing Infosys to constantly innovate to maintain its premium pricing.

## 4. Open Questions
*   **Long-term Visa Impact:** While the company claims no "material impact" from the current PERM suspension, investors should monitor if the situation escalates or if costs to acquire local talent in Western markets significantly erode profitability over the next 12–18 months.
*   **Growth Trajectory:** Will the adoption of Generative AI lead to new, high-margin revenue growth, or will it cannibalize existing high-revenue legacy service projects?
*   **Valuation:** At a P/E of ~14.8, is the stock reflecting a "value" trap due to slowing growth expectations, or is it an attractive entry point for a long-term compounder?
