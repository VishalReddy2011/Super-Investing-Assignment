# Sarvottam Cables (NSE: SRVCABLE) - Equity Research Brief
*As of 23 September 2026*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors, catering primarily to the power utility and data-centre infrastructure sectors. For Q1 FY27, the company reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore (up 18% YoY) and a net profit of ₹82 crore. The company is currently expanding capacity via a new facility in Bharuch, Gujarat, expected to come online in Q3 FY27.

## 2. Bull Case
*   **Strong Demand Pipeline:** The company’s order book has grown to ₹3,900 crore (up from ₹3,150 crore YoY), signaling sustained demand from power transmission and data-centre projects.
*   **Strategic Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch plant is 70% complete and will increase cable production capacity by approximately 30%, supporting the management's FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17%.
*   **Improving Shareholder Governance:** Contrary to 2024 reports, promoter pledging has significantly declined from a peak of 35% to just 4.1% of their holding as of June 2026, reducing systemic risk for minority shareholders.
*   **Institutional Confidence:** FII and DII holdings have shown a steady upward trend over the past year, increasing to 11.8% and 14.6% respectively.

## 3. Bear Case
*   **Margin Compression:** EBITDA margins contracted by 170 bps to 11.2% in Q1 due to a 14% spike in copper costs. While the company has price-variation clauses, these come with a 1–2 quarter lag, creating short-term volatility in profitability.
*   **Working Capital Pressures:** Receivable days have deteriorated from 78 to 96 days, driven by delayed payments from two large state utility customers. 
*   **Regulatory & Tax Overhangs:** The company faces a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore for FY2020-22, which it is currently contesting.
*   **Note on Misidentification:** Investors should distinguish this company from "Sarvottam Cable Network," a local cable TV operator recently penalized for illegal wiring; the two entities are distinct.

## 4. Open Questions
*   **Revenue Discrepancy:** The press release states Q1 revenue at ₹1,248 crore, while the *Business Daily* report cites ₹1,428 crore. Investors should verify the official audited results on the NSE portal to confirm the accurate figure.
*   **GST Litigation:** Will the ₹46.3 crore GST demand require a cash deposit or bank guarantee to file an appeal, and how will this impact liquidity if the appeal is unsuccessful?
*   **Recovery Timeline:** How quickly can the company normalize receivable days, and is there a risk of further elongation from state-run utilities?
